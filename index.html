Enterprise Fault Logging Audit Portal

Equinix Singapore internship · Power Apps, Power Automate, SharePoint, Excel, Microsoft Teams

An internal audit portal that replaced a manual routine of typing errors, sending photos by chat, renaming files, copying links and updating personal tracking files.

What I built

  • Three Power Apps modules: error registration, SME review console and error rectification.
  • Nine Power Automate flows for submission, deletion, status updates, reminders and rectification.
  • Multi-photo fault reporting stored in SharePoint, linked to Excel records by a unique Serial ID.
  • A status lifecycle (Pending, Sent for Correction, Rectified, Closed) with Microsoft Teams notifications.
  • Notification links that open the app with the record details already filled in.

Problems I solved

  • Speed: searching the whole multi-tab Excel file took about 40 seconds per submission. I routed each record to its monthly table instead.
  • Data quality: each Serial ID is checked against Excel before saving, and unknown IDs are rejected with a clear message.
  • Data consistency: every record has a unique ID, and deleting a file or folder also removes its matching Excel rows.
  • Access control: users can only delete their own submissions.
  • Handover: I wrote full technical documentation of the system and its flows.
About 62 hours saved per year (estimate) From about 74 hours to 11 hours of manual work, based on 10 errors per week (520 per year).
